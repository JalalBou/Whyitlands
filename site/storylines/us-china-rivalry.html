US and China, tariffs and truces

Washington uses access to its market as leverage over Beijing, and Beijing answers with its control of rare earths; neither wants a rupture, so they trade short truces. When the Supreme Court removed the emergency tariff tool, Washington rebuilt its tariffs on other statutes, but the duty-free parcel did not come back.

Our read

Our reading: the truces are getting shorter and cover bulk trade in non-sensitive goods, not the parcel, so planning should assume no return of duty-free entry to the United States whatever happens at APEC. Each expiry date, now 10 January 2027, is a repricing date for transpacific capacity and for any contract priced on current tariffs.
