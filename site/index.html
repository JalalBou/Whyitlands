The why

From geopolitics to the parcel

Middle East

Hormuz puts a fuel bill on every air parcel

What happened
Brent has been above $100 since the war began in February.
Why
Integrators and Asia–Europe air freight pass it on through fuel surcharges.
What it means
Cross-border margins are squeezed just before peak season.
North America

The $800 free pass is gone. US clearance now runs on data

What happened
The de minimis exemption has been suspended indefinitely since 24 June 2026.
Why
Duties are now paid monthly by a designated filer, usually the operator.
What it means
An edge for those who master customs data: classification, origin, value.
China

A trade truce, but no return to direct small parcels

What happened
Tariff cuts on about $30bn of goods each way in September.
Why
The US de minimis exemption is not restored by the truce.
What it means
Platforms keep moving stock into local warehouses.
