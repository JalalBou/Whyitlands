ASEAN parcels: J&T and the platforms take the volume, everyone else takes the hit

Southeast Asian e-commerce kept growing at 20% plus in 2025 and parcel volumes rose even faster in 2026, but the gains are concentrating in J&T Express and platform-run logistics. Independents, posts and Vietnamese incumbents are posting thin or falling profits while platforms and carriers pass fuel and logistics costs on to sellers.

Our read

ASEAN parcels have become a contest between two models: platform-controlled logistics (SPX, and TikTok Shop's managed carrier network) and one neutral carrier with the lowest costs (J&T). Volumes are growing quickly, around 47% in H1 2026 on J&T's estimate, but the value goes to whoever controls order flow or has the lowest cost per parcel. Mid-sized independents, the posts and Vietnam's incumbents are growing revenue while profits shrink, and they are moving into B2B, cold chain, premium or cross-border niches. Platforms are passing logistics costs to sellers through fees, so marketplace sellers are effectively paying for the faster delivery. Governments are tightening on low-value imports and below-cost selling at the same time, which narrows the direct parcel channel from China and favours goods stocked in the region.

Battlegrounds

Where the competition is fought

Platforms decide who gets the parcels

Shopee and TikTok Shop hold nearly 98% of Vietnamese e-commerce value, and Vietnamese carriers say buyers see only part of the carriers that qualify for an order. Flash Express left Malaysia in January 2026 citing platform self-preferencing, while SPX now sells capacity to outside users in Singapore and Vietnam. J&T grew Southeast Asian volume 71% in H1 2026 as a core partner of all three platforms. Our reading: in ASEAN, access to marketplace volume now matters more than price or service quality, and an independent courier without a platform anchor struggles to stay at scale.3614433249

The price war has become a cost war

J&T's Southeast Asian revenue works out at about US$0.54 per parcel in H1 2026 (our calculation from reported revenue and volume). When fuel spiked in spring 2026, carriers added surcharges rather than absorbing the cost: 3 baht per parcel in Thailand, 15% at Viettel Post and RM1.95/kg at Ninja Van in East Malaysia. Viettel Post's H1 profit still fell 33% and GHN's parent fell 94.5%. Our reading: only operators with the lowest cost per parcel make money at today's rates, and the rest are relying on surcharges that platforms and sellers resist.6515331135

Platforms push logistics costs onto sellers

In Indonesia, TikTok Shop and Tokopedia began charging sellers a logistics service fee on 1 May 2026, and from June they added charges for failed deliveries and returns. Shopee raised its Gratis Ongkir XTRA free-shipping fees on 2 May, and in Vietnam it adjusted its PiShip shipping service fee from 23 May. Shopee's take rate rose from 8.6% in 2022 to 13.5% at end 2025, and sellers report effective cuts near 30% including logistics. Our reading: platforms are funding free shipping and faster delivery out of seller margins, which keeps buyer prices low but drives sellers towards fulfilment programmes run by the platforms.1819202142

Consolidation through exits and repositioning rather than mergers

No large parcel merger in ASEAN was found for June to September 2026. Instead, Flash Express has left Malaysia, Ninja Van raised money at roughly half its 2021 valuation, Lazada cut about 5% of staff, and KEX repositioned itself towards end-to-end and premium logistics. Southeast Asian logistics-tech funding reached US$339m in 2026 to date, the highest since 2021 but well below 2021's US$1.1bn. Our reading: with capital scarce, weaker networks are likely to shrink, sell or specialise rather than be bought outright.1310381650

Regulators close the small-parcel channel

Since 1 January 2026, Thailand has charged import duty on online imports from 1 baht (previously exempt up to 1,500 baht), with Lazada, Shopee, Shein, TikTok Shop and Temu collecting. Vietnam ended its VAT exemption on express imports under VND1m in February 2025, and its e-commerce law in force since 1 July 2026 requires foreign platforms with no Vietnamese presence to post a bank deposit. Indonesia's Permendag 19/2026 sets a US$100 FOB minimum per unit for cross-border goods sold on platforms and requires platforms to curb below-cost selling. Our reading: the regulatory direction in ASEAN mirrors the US and EU ending de minimis, which favours goods stocked in-country over direct parcels from China.2324252622

Chinese supply keeps arriving, but through local sellers

China's exports to Southeast Asia rose 30.2% year on year in August 2026. Momentum Works notes Shopee and TikTok Shop are already 'full of Chinese sellers' drawing on the same supply pool as Temu, whose 2025 Philippine GMV it estimates at no more than US$50m. ASEAN DEFA, concluded in May 2026 and due to be signed in Manila in November, aims to harmonise e-commerce, payments and data rules. Our reading: rather than a flood of direct small parcels, the flows redirected from the US and EU are coming in through locally registered sellers and warehouses, which benefits domestic carriers and fulfilment providers more than cross-border postal lanes.41422728

Country by country

Six markets, six rulebooks

The first line of each card is our reading; open it for the sourced facts.

Indonesia

Our reading Indonesia is the region's largest parcel pool, but platform GMV has nearly stopped growing, so carriers fight for share of a flat base while the platforms decide routing. The KPPU review shows that a behavioural remedy has not moved volume away from SPX, and the courier-discount rule protects tariffs only where platforms are not paying.
Size
E-commerce GMV about US$71bn in 2025 (+14%), within a digital economy near US$100bn (e-Conomy SEA). Indonesia is about 37% of regional platform GMV, but Momentum Works puts its 2025 platform growth at only 2.2%. The courier association says daily shipments can exceed 30m parcels.
Leaders
Shopee, and TikTok Shop with Tokopedia (TikTok Shop Indonesia GMV US$13.1bn in 2025). A KPPU survey published in July 2026 gives SPX 48.5% of Shopee deliveries on the sellers' side and 61.5% on the buyers' side, and found J&T's probability of selection 98.2% lower on Shopee transactions. J&T's Indonesian 'Double 6' volume rose 112% in June 2026.
Dynamics
Platforms are moving logistics costs to sellers: TikTok Shop and Tokopedia logistics fees from 1 May 2026, failed-delivery and return charges from June, and higher Shopee free-shipping programme fees from 2 May. KPPU halted its SPX self-preferencing case after Shopee committed to change its courier algorithm, but its July 2026 review found the algorithm still steers buyers to SPX and that only about five of roughly 700 licensed couriers can operate effectively on Shopee.
Rules that bite
Permenkomdigi 8/2025 (May 2025) limits courier discounts that take tariffs below cost to three days a month; platform-funded free shipping is outside its scope. Permendag 19/2026 (June 2026) sets a US$100 FOB minimum per unit for cross-border goods sold on platforms, requires fee transparency and seller consent to fee changes, and targets below-cost selling.535155485657441819205422

Vietnam

Our reading Vietnam has the fastest platform sales growth among the larger ASEAN markets, but two platforms control routing, so the incumbents' revenue lags the market and their margins are falling. They are spending on sorting, B2B and cold chain to earn returns outside marketplace volume.
Size
E-commerce GMV US$25bn in 2025 (+17%) within a US$39bn digital economy (e-Conomy SEA). Sales on the four main platforms reached VND291.6tn in H1 2026 (+44.1%). The postal sector handled about 4.1bn items in 2025, more than 90% of them parcels, for revenue above VND87,000bn.
Leaders
Shopee held 54.5% and TikTok Shop 43.4% of H1 2026 e-commerce value. Viettel Post claims 22.3% of 2025 delivery revenue, with GHN, GHTK, VNPost and J&T each between 12.4% and 15.7%; SPX carries Shopee volume and serves individual senders through pickup points.
Dynamics
Incumbents grow revenue but lose profit: Viettel Post's H1 2026 revenue rose 2% to VND10,230bn while profit fell 33%, despite a 15% fuel surcharge from April, and GHN's parent SCommerce saw profit fall 94.5% as debt rose 85.1% to fund sorting centres. Temu suspended sales on 4 December 2024 after failing to complete registration with the Ministry of Industry and Trade.
Rules that bite
VAT on express imports under VND1m since February 2025. Since 1 July 2025 platforms withhold VAT and personal income tax for individual sellers (Decree 117/2025). The e-commerce law in force from 1 July 2026 requires foreign platforms without a local presence to post a deposit. An amended Postal Law with risk-based parcel management is due before the National Assembly in October 2026.58615960363732333562246325

Thailand

Our reading Thailand pairs the region's fastest e-commerce growth with its tightest low-value import regime, which pushes Chinese supply towards locally stocked sellers. The parcel market has already shed weaker players, and the uniform fuel surcharge in April suggests the remaining carriers have regained some pricing power.
Size
E-commerce GMV US$33bn in 2025 (+22%, the fastest in the region on e-Conomy SEA figures); Momentum Works puts 2025 platform GMV growth at 51.8%. The express parcel market was about 100bn baht in 2024 (+12%), at 10 to 12m parcels a day.
Leaders
Shopee Thailand reported 2024 revenue of 49.96bn baht and Lazada Thailand 30.16bn baht. Thailand Post leads in parcels, Flash Express is second and J&T grows on TikTok Shop volume; Thailand Post carries only about 1 to 2% of TikTok deliveries. J&T's Thai 'Double 6' volume rose 89% in June 2026.
Dynamics
Exits and repositioning: SCG closed SCG Express after losses in every year from 2019 to 2023, and KEX's revenue fell from 17,145m baht in 2022 to 9,616m baht in 2024 as its loss widened to 5,991m baht, before delisting and a move to premium logistics. On 1 April 2026 J&T, KEX and Flash added 3 baht per parcel for fuel.
Rules that bite
7% VAT on imports valued at or below 1,500 baht since 5 July 2024. From 1 January 2026, import duty applies from 1 baht, with Lazada, Shopee, Shein, TikTok Shop and Temu collecting, on an estimated 250m parcels a year. A draft Postal Business Act would license private carriers and create a regulator covering rates and service standards.6451656867446616156923

Philippines

Our reading The Philippines is the main ASEAN market where low-value direct imports remain largely untaxed, which keeps the cross-border parcel lane open for now. A lower de minimis would follow the regional pattern and is the main regulatory risk to that lane; domestically, inter-island reach is what separates carriers.
Size
E-commerce GMV US$24bn in 2025, up from US$20bn, within a US$36bn digital economy (+16%); video commerce is 25% of e-commerce GMV (e-Conomy SEA). Momentum Works records platform GMV growth above 20% in 2025.
Leaders
Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop; TikTok Shop says its local sellers' sales grew 200% in 2025. LBC Express returned to a P246.06m profit in 2025 on service revenue down 1.89% to P14.03bn. Lalamove extended on-demand delivery to eight Mindanao cities in September 2026.
Dynamics
Platforms added per-order seller fees: Shopee and Lazada from 3 September 2025, TikTok Shop from 1 December 2025 (P5 per order, charged at P3 initially). Growth is spreading beyond Luzon, with TikTok Shop and Lalamove both targeting the Visayas and Mindanao in 2026.
Rules that bite
The de minimis remains PHP10,000, free of duty and tax; the Philippine Retailers Association estimates P57bn of imports a year enter tax-free under it, and the Finance Department said in May 2025 it was reviewing the rule. CAO 01-2025 requires accreditation of platforms, e-retailers and forwarders and sets up a dedicated e-commerce clearance system. RA 12023 has applied 12% VAT to non-resident digital service providers since 2 June 2025.70517273407176757477

Malaysia

Our reading Malaysia is the clearest duopoly in ASEAN parcels, and Flash's exit shows the cost of operating without platform volume. A mandatory floor price would lift yields for smaller carriers but would not remove the two leaders' cost and order-flow advantage.
Size
E-commerce GMV US$20bn in 2025 (+21%) within a US$39bn digital economy (+19%, the region's fastest); Momentum Works puts 2025 platform GMV growth at 47.6%. Parcel volume passed 899m by Q3 2024, against 52m in 2015, and the courier market was projected at RM6.8bn to RM6.9bn for 2025.
Leaders
J&T Express and SPX Xpress hold 73.5% of the domestic courier market, and 15 of 102 licensees carry 98% of parcels. J&T Malaysia earned RM69.94m in FY2024.
Dynamics
Flash Express closed on 31 January 2026, citing platform self-preferencing. Pos Malaysia lost RM132.17m in the nine months to September 2025 and GDEX remains loss-making; Ninja Van Malaysia cut its loss to RM3.41m in FY2024 and added a RM1.95/kg fuel surcharge on East Malaysia lanes from 13 April 2026.
Rules that bite
10% sales tax on imported goods of RM500 or less sold online since 1 January 2024, with sellers registering above RM500,000 of such sales a year. MCMC's RM5 reference price for parcels up to 2kg is non-binding; in November 2025 the government said it was studying a mandatory floor price.78518079818213144783

Singapore

Our reading Singapore is a small, mature parcel market where growth comes from platforms and resale marketplaces rather than new buyers. Its tax regime is settled, so competition turns on reliability at peaks and on access to drop-off networks rather than on price.
Size
Digital economy US$29bn in 2025 (+7%, e-Conomy SEA); Momentum Works records platform GMV growth above 20%. SingPost delivered 7.1m domestic parcels in the quarter to June 2026 (+36.5%).
Leaders
SingPost, Ninja Van and the platform networks. SPX joined Carousell's delivery options in April 2026, adding more than 1,400 drop-off points. Lazada, whose regional GMV Cube estimates fell 28% in 2025, cut about 5% of its staff, including in Singapore, in June 2026.
Dynamics
Ninja Van apologised in May 2026 for delays of four to eight days or more, which it blamed on an unexpected rise in volume. SingPost sold its Australian business at A$1.02bn enterprise value (announced December 2024) and is investing S$30m in automated parcel sortation.
Rules that bite
GST on imported low-value goods (S$400 or less) has been collected at the point of sale through the Overseas Vendor Registration regime since 1 January 2023; the GST rate has been 9% since January 2024.84513043883812878586

SPX Express (Shopee, Sea Ltd)

Platform logistics

Shopee Q2 2026: GMV US$38.3bn (+28.4%), 4.2bn gross orders (+27.5%); Indonesian instant-delivery orders +80% with cost per order down about 20%; fulfilment orders +20% quarter on quarter.

Strategy Sea runs its own first-to-last-mile network so that Shopee controls delivery speed and cost per order. SPX is also selling capacity outside Shopee, for instance through Carousell in Singapore and, in Vietnam, through more than 100 pickup points serving individual senders since Q3 2025.

Latest moves April 2026: SPX added to Carousell Official Delivery, giving more than 1,400 drop-off points in Singapore. August 2026: Sea reported faster-growing fulfilment and 1-hour instant delivery in urban areas.

Under pressure from Sea's Q2 2026 cost of revenue rose 51.9%, mainly logistics costs and investment, and value-added services revenue fell 9% as more revenue was netted against shipping subsidies.784332

J&T Express

Express

Southeast Asia H1 2026: 5.52bn parcels (+71.2%), 30.5m a day, 38.1% share, revenue about US$3.0bn (+63.8%); 127 sorting centres and 75 automated lines at end Q2 2026. Group adjusted EBIT per parcel US$0.025 (+77%).

Strategy A core carrier for TikTok Shop, Lazada and Shopee at the same time, it is adding automated sorting capacity; our reading is that it is positioning as the low-cost neutral network. It is also starting to target non-platform shippers, which management describes as still at an early stage.

Latest moves June 2026: 213m parcels in the 'Double 6' campaign (+83.5%), with Indonesia +112% and Thailand +89%. August 2026: capital expenditure to be weighted towards automation in Thailand and Vietnam.

Under pressure from Our calculation puts its Southeast Asian revenue per parcel at about US$0.54 in H1 2026, so margins depend on cost per parcel continuing to drop, and on platforms that also run their own carriers.4539446

TikTok Shop / Tokopedia

Platform logistics

TikTok Shop SEA platform GMV US$45.6bn in 2025 (doubled); with Tokopedia it reached 65.7% of Shopee's regional GMV. In Vietnam, 43.4% of e-commerce value in H1 2026 against Shopee's 54.5%.

Strategy Rather than building its own courier fleet, TikTok Shop runs logistics through partner carriers, with J&T named its strategic partner in Vietnam since 2024. It is now charging sellers for logistics directly instead of absorbing the cost.

Latest moves 1 May 2026: an Indonesian seller logistics service fee, capped at Rp5,055 per order on TikTok Shop and Rp10,110 on Tokopedia. 1 June 2026: sellers charged up to Rp5,000 for each failed delivery and each buyer-preference return, with fees waived for three months and the system active from 1 September.

Under pressure from Sellers' total fees keep rising, while new Indonesian rules require fee transparency, seller consent to fee changes and monitoring of below-cost selling.482493632181922

Lazada Logistics (LEX) and Cainiao

Platform logistics

Lazada is the third of the three platforms that together hold 98.8% of regional platform GMV. Cainiao runs more than 20 warehousing facilities across 10 Asia-Pacific markets, six of them in Southeast Asia.

Strategy LEX remains Lazada's in-house logistics arm, while Alibaba's Cainiao sells warehousing and fulfilment to cross-border sellers. Lazada is cutting costs to defend its position in markets where it is doing well, such as Singapore, Thailand and the Philippines.

Latest moves 23 June 2026: Lazada cut about 5% of its Southeast Asian workforce, including Singapore, citing organisational efficiency.

Under pressure from Our reading: it is losing ground in volume to Shopee and TikTok Shop, which limits the scale its logistics arm can reach.251463832

Next 12 months

Three scenarios

Our reading, grounded in the facts above. The likelihood labels are judgements, not probabilities.

most likely

Two-network consolidation continues

J&T and the platform-run networks keep taking share through the 11.11 and 12.12 peaks and into 2027, with regional volume growth staying well above GMV growth. Platforms keep adding seller fees rather than raising buyer prices, and mid-sized independents shrink, specialise or leave individual markets rather than merge. Posts and Vietnamese incumbents grow revenue slowly while profits stay under pressure.

Watch for J&T Q3 and Q4 2026 updates showing Southeast Asian volume growth above 40%; further platform fee notices to sellers; another carrier leaving a market or a business line; Sea's Q3 2026 results showing logistics cost per order still falling.

plausible

Regulators rebalance platforms and carriers

Competition and postal regulators turn studies into binding rules: a mandatory courier floor price in Malaysia, enforced algorithm neutrality in Indonesia, licensing and rate oversight under new postal laws in Vietnam and Thailand. Independent carriers regain some volume and yield on marketplaces, platform networks grow more slowly, and sellers pay more. A lower Philippine de minimis would close the last large untaxed low-value lane.

Watch for Vietnam's National Assembly passing the amended Postal Law; a Malaysian decision on a mandatory floor price; KPPU reopening or extending its Shopee courier case; progress on Thailand's Postal Business Act; a Philippine Finance Department proposal to lower the PHP10,000 threshold; ASEAN DEFA signature in November 2026.

tail risk

Price war returns

A demand slowdown or a well-funded new push, for instance a Chinese platform or carrier building local networks to place goods redirected from the US and EU, restarts below-cost pricing. Fuel surcharges are withdrawn, revenue per parcel falls faster than cost per parcel, and losses spread from independents to the leaders, forcing a second round of exits and possibly the first large merger.

Watch for J&T revenue per parcel falling faster than its cost per parcel; carriers withdrawing fuel surcharges while fuel stays high; a large funding round or acquisition by a new entrant; Chinese exports to ASEAN continuing to grow by 25% or more year on year.

So what

What it means for you

Merchants / platforms

Budget for logistics as a platform fee line that keeps rising (logistics service fees, failed-delivery and return charges, free-shipping programme fees) rather than as a carrier rate you negotiate. Selling direct or through several channels is the main way to keep a choice of carrier, since on the marketplaces the platforms decide which carriers buyers see.

Cross-border operators into ASEAN

Indonesia's US$100 FOB minimum, Thailand's duty from 1 baht and Vietnam's VAT and foreign-platform deposit rules make direct low-value parcels costlier and riskier. Plans should move towards bulk import, local registration and in-country stock, with duties and taxes collected at checkout.

Carriers

Matching J&T or SPX on cost per parcel requires automation and scale that few others can fund. The options left are to become a platform's dependable overflow partner, to specialise (premium, bulky, cold chain, B2B, on-demand) or to merge. Fuel surcharges stick only where the carrier still has pricing power.

EmergingConfidence mediumSoutheast Asia

ASEAN parcel margins now come from pass-through, not price cuts

Platforms and carriers in ASEAN are protecting margins by passing logistics and fuel costs on to sellers, while unit revenue per parcel keeps falling.

Our reading Revenue of about US$0.54 per parcel (our calculation) alongside higher profit per parcel and new seller fees shows the price war has not ended but has changed who pays. Buyers still see free or cheap shipping, while sellers carry more of the cost through platform fees and surcharges. Merchants should expect logistics to keep rising as a share of their platform fees in the 2026 peak, and carriers without scale efficiency cannot copy this model.

Platform-gated volume pushes ASEAN independents into retreat

Because platforms control which carriers sellers and buyers can use, independent ASEAN couriers are exiting, specialising or losing profit even though the market is growing about 47% a year.

Our reading Volumes are growing quickly, yet independent operators outside the platform-linked networks are losing profit or leaving. Taken together, this suggests the main constraint is access to platform order flow, not demand or price. For sellers, a shrinking pool of independents means less leverage on off-platform shipping. For operators, the realistic paths are to become a platform's dependable partner, to specialise, or to consolidate.
